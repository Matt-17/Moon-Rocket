# Moon Rocket 🚀

Fly your rocket over a living stock chart and see how far the market takes you. HODL to thrust, release to drop — dodge the candles, grab diamonds, and ride the news.

## How to Play

- **Hold** (mouse, touch, or spacebar) to fire the thruster, **release** to fall
- The chart scrolls faster the longer you survive — your score is the **floor** you reach
- 💎 **Diamonds** float in the chart: collect them to unlock new rocket skins
- 📰 **News pickups** appear in weekend gaps — a collected headline reshapes the following week. Calm news flattens the chart, bad news makes it ugly. Learn which ones to grab!

## Daily Challenge

Every day there is one seeded market — **every player flies the exact same chart**. Compete on the daily leaderboard, or switch to Random mode for a fresh market every run. Yesterday's challenge comes back as a replayable recap post with the final results.

## Compete

- **Daily, high score, and diamond leaderboards** right in the post
- Only your best diamond haul of the day is credited — no farming
- Milestone flairs for the community: reach floor 100, go **🚀 Mooning** at 500, earn **💎🙌 Diamond Hands** at 1000

## For Moderators

Install the app and use the subreddit menu:

- **Create Moon Rocket Game Post** — adds a game post to your community
- **Create Daily Recap Post** — posts yesterday's results with a replayable archive

Each community keeps its own leaderboards and flairs.
